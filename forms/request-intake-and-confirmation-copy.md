# Request Intake and Confirmation Copy

## Purpose

The request and inquiry forms should collect validation data for the website MVP. The site is testing whether visitors submit requests, questions, and resource-download actions. The copy should not promise immediate follow-up or guaranteed service.

## Recommended primary CTA

> Submit a project inquiry or quote request. We review submissions to understand current radiation safety needs and may follow up if the project appears to be a good fit.

## Recommended secondary CTAs

> Not ready to submit a project? Submit a radiation safety question.

> Looking for a starting point? Download a free checklist or template.

> Want future resources? Join the update list.

## Copy to avoid

Avoid:

- We will respond within one business day.
- Schedule a free consultation.
- Book a call now.
- Immediate support available.
- We will contact you shortly.
- We accept all projects.

## Follow-up priority categories

- High: clear commercial project, strong fit, near-term need.
- Medium: useful potential project but unclear scope or timing.
- Low: general question or low-fit inquiry.
- Content-only: useful question for future article or free resource.
- Ignore/spam: not relevant.
