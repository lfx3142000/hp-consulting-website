# RSO Inspection Readiness Checklist
**Advantage Health Physics | Free Resource**

Use this checklist to evaluate your radiation safety program's readiness before an NRC or Agreement State inspection. Work through each section and note any gaps that need to be addressed.

---

## Section 1: License Documents and Records
- [ ] Current license or registration is on file and accessible
- [ ] License conditions are understood by the RSO and facility management
- [ ] Authorized users are listed and current
- [ ] Authorized use areas are identified and match the license
- [ ] License amendments are current and on file
- [ ] Renewal dates are tracked and submissions are not overdue

## Section 2: Radiation Safety Program Documentation
- [ ] Written Radiation Safety Program or Radiation Protection Program is current
- [ ] RSO duties and authority are clearly defined in writing
- [ ] Radiation safety procedures are written, approved, and accessible
- [ ] All procedures are reviewed and updated within the required time period
- [ ] ALARA program is documented

## Section 3: Personnel Training and Qualification
- [ ] All authorized users and workers have completed required initial training
- [ ] Annual or refresher training is current for all applicable personnel
- [ ] Training records are maintained and available for review
- [ ] Visitors and contractors who enter restricted areas have received applicable instruction

## Section 4: Survey Instruments and Calibration
- [ ] All required survey instruments are available and functional
- [ ] Calibration records are current and within required intervals
- [ ] Calibration is performed by an accredited or qualified provider
- [ ] Instruments are labeled with calibration date and due date
- [ ] Constancy checks are performed and documented as required

## Section 5: Radiation Surveys
- [ ] Area surveys are performed at required frequencies
- [ ] Survey records are maintained with date, surveyor, instrument ID, and results
- [ ] Action levels are defined and documented
- [ ] Surveys following use, spills, or repairs are recorded

## Section 6: Radioactive Material Inventory and Accountability
- [ ] Current inventory of all licensed radioactive material is maintained
- [ ] Sealed sources are inventoried at required intervals (typically every 6 months)
- [ ] Leak tests are performed and current for sealed sources requiring testing
- [ ] Disposal records are complete and accurate
- [ ] Transfer records are maintained

## Section 7: Dosimetry and Exposure Records
- [ ] Occupational dosimetry is provided to all workers meeting monitoring criteria
- [ ] Dosimetry vendor is accredited by NVLAP
- [ ] Dose records are maintained and available for inspection
- [ ] ALARA investigations are documented if applicable thresholds are exceeded
- [ ] Declared pregnant workers have been identified and accommodated per program requirements

## Section 8: Postings and Labeling
- [ ] All radiation areas, high radiation areas, and restricted areas are properly posted
- [ ] Radioactive material containers are labeled with required information
- [ ] NRC Form 3 (or equivalent) is posted
- [ ] Emergency contact information is posted

## Section 9: Incidents, Overexposures, and Corrective Actions
- [ ] All reportable events have been reported to the appropriate authority
- [ ] Incident reports are on file
- [ ] Root cause analyses and corrective actions are documented for events
- [ ] Corrective action effectiveness has been evaluated

## Section 10: Management Review and Audits
- [ ] Annual radiation safety program audit has been performed
- [ ] Audit report and corrective actions are on file
- [ ] RSO has reported program status to management within the required period
- [ ] Management audit sign-off is documented

---

## How to Use This Checklist
1. Work through each section with your RSO or radiation safety staff.
2. Mark items as complete, incomplete, or not applicable.
3. For each incomplete item, assign a responsible party and target completion date.
4. Resolve all findings before your next scheduled or anticipated inspection.

## Note
This checklist is a general reference tool. License requirements vary by license type, materials, and jurisdiction. Consult your specific license conditions and applicable NRC or Agreement State regulations.
