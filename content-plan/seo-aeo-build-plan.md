## Regulatory source map for future AEO pages

Use official sources when drafting regulatory pages. For New England radioactive material licensing questions, remember that all six New England states are Agreement States as of the current research date. Agreement State licensees generally file radioactive material applications with the state regulator, not with NRC, unless the applicant is a federal agency, federally recognized Indian tribe, or otherwise under retained NRC jurisdiction.

Primary federal sources to check before drafting answers:

- NRC Agreement States page: `https://www.nrc.gov/agreement-states`
- NRC Agreement State Program: `https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/state-tribal/agreement-states`
- 10 CFR Part 19 — Notices, instructions, and reports to workers; inspection and investigations.
- 10 CFR Part 20 — Standards for protection against radiation.
- 10 CFR Part 30 — General domestic licensing of byproduct material.
- 10 CFR Part 31 — General domestic licenses for byproduct material.
- 10 CFR Part 32 — Specific domestic licenses to manufacture or transfer certain items containing byproduct material.
- 10 CFR Part 33 — Specific domestic licenses of broad scope.
- 10 CFR Part 34 — Industrial radiography.
- 10 CFR Part 35 — Medical use of byproduct material.
- 10 CFR Part 36 — Irradiators.
- 10 CFR Part 37 — Physical protection of category 1 and category 2 quantities of radioactive material.
- 10 CFR Part 39 — Well logging.
- 10 CFR Part 40 — Source material.
- 10 CFR Part 70 — Special nuclear material.
- 10 CFR Part 71 — Packaging and transportation of radioactive material.
- 10 CFR Part 150 — Exemptions and continued NRC authority in Agreement States.

New England state source starting points:

- Massachusetts: NRC Massachusetts Agreement State page; Massachusetts Division of Radiation Control; 105 CMR 120.
- Connecticut: NRC Connecticut Agreement State page; Connecticut DEEP Radiation Division; Connecticut radiation regulations. Note that Connecticut became an Agreement State on 10/1/2025 and some licensing/reciprocity/inspection web content may still be developing.
- Maine: NRC Maine Agreement State page; Maine DHHS Radiological Control Program; Maine radiation protection rules.
- New Hampshire: NRC New Hampshire Agreement State page; New Hampshire DHHS Radiological Health Section; New Hampshire radiation rules.
- Rhode Island: NRC Rhode Island Agreement State page; Rhode Island Department of Health radiation control; Rhode Island radiation regulations.
- Vermont: NRC Vermont Agreement State page; Vermont Office of Radiological Health; Vermont radiation control rules.
