# SEO and AEO Build Plan

## Objective

Use the Advantage Health Physics website to test organic search demand. The site should attract people searching for health physics, radiation safety, RSO support, radioactive material licensing, audits, training, surveys, shielding, instrumentation, and compliance topics.

The site should collect validation signals, not promise immediate consulting delivery.

## Primary conversion actions

1. Submit a project inquiry or quote request.
2. Submit a radiation safety question.
3. Download a free checklist or template.
4. Join the update list.

## Priority audience segments

- Radiation Safety Officers.
- EHS managers.
- Nuclear medicine departments.
- Universities and research labs.
- Radiopharmaceutical and biotech teams.
- Industrial users of sealed sources or radiation devices.
- Small licensees that need practical program support.
- Organizations preparing for an inspection or license amendment.

## AEO page format

Every major page should include a direct answer near the top.

Example:

```text
## Direct answer
A radiation safety program audit reviews whether a licensee's procedures, training, surveys, records, dosimetry, waste controls, and corrective actions match applicable requirements and the facility's approved license. A mock inspection can help identify gaps before a regulator inspection.
```

Then add practical sections:

- Who this is for.
- When this matters.
- Typical records to review.
- Common mistakes.
- FAQ.
- Related resources.
- Request/question CTA.

## FAQ strategy

Add 4-8 FAQs to every major service and article page. Good FAQ types:

- What is it?
- Who needs it?
- When is it required?
- What records are needed?
- What causes problems?
- How often should it be reviewed?
- What should a small licensee do first?
- How can a visitor submit a question?

## Internal linking map

Each page should have four link types:

1. Service link.
2. Topic hub link.
3. Related article link.
4. Conversion link.

Example:

- Article: How to Prepare for an NRC Inspection.
- Service link: Radiation Safety Audits and Mock NRC Inspections.
- Hub link: Audits and Inspections.
- Related article: How to Read an NRC Inspection Report.
- Conversion link: Download Audit Checklist or Submit Project Inquiry.

## Measurement

Use Search Console to identify:

- Queries with impressions but low clicks.
- Pages with clicks but no conversions.
- Topics with rising impressions.
- Questions that deserve new pages.

Use form/download tracking to identify:

- Highest-demand service categories.
- Facility types showing interest.
- States/regions showing interest.
- Most useful free resources.

## Monthly decision rule

Build more pages only where data shows interest. If a topic gets impressions, clicks, questions, downloads, or inquiries, expand it. If a topic gets no traction after indexing and internal linking, deprioritize it.
