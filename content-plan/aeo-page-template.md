# AEO Page Template — Advantage Health Physics

## Purpose
This template is used to create Answer Engine Optimized (AEO) pages that target specific regulatory and compliance questions in health physics and radiation safety. Each page should answer one primary question directly at the top, then provide structured supporting content for depth and credibility.

## When to use this template
- A specific regulatory question has meaningful search volume or AI answer surface area
- The topic maps to a service Advantage Health Physics offers
- A free resource (checklist, template) can support the page
- The page fits one of the eight SEO content clusters

---

## Page metadata (fill in for every new page)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Suggested slug | `/topic-keyword-phrase` |
| Page title (H1) | Question or answer phrasing, 50–60 chars |
| Meta title | Include primary keyword, under 60 chars |
| Meta description | 140–155 chars, answers the question directly, includes CTA |
| Primary keyword | Single focus phrase (e.g. "RSO inspection checklist") |

| Jurisdiction note | NRC, MA, CT, RI, NH, VT, ME, or All New England |

## Page structure

### 1. Direct answer block (top of page, above the fold)

Write 2–3 sentences that answer the primary question directly. This block should be usable as a standalone answer by AI search engines. Do not use vague language — be specific and cite the regulatory basis.

**Example:**
> Under 10 CFR 35.70, licensees must survey areas where radiopharmaceuticals are routinely prepared or administered at the end of each day of use. Areas where radioactive material is stored must be surveyed weekly.

---

### 2. Who this is for

Describe the specific audience: what type of facility, what role, what situation they are in when they need this information.

---

### 3. When this matters

Describe the trigger scenarios — license application, upcoming inspection, new procedure, equipment change, regulatory update, etc.

---

### 4. Regulatory basis

List the specific regulations, guidance documents, and inspection procedures that govern this topic. Include CFR section numbers and NRC document identifiers where applicable.

- **Primary regulation:** [10 CFR XX.XX or Agreement State equivalent]
- **NRC guidance:** [NUREG number or inspection procedure]
- **Jurisdiction note:** [NRC, Agreement State, or both]

---

### 5. Step-by-step or checklist section

Provide an actionable breakdown: steps, checklist items, or decision criteria. Use a numbered list or checkbox-style formatting. This section should be the most useful and quotable part of the page.

---

### 6. Common mistakes

List 3–5 common compliance gaps or misunderstandings related to this topic. This increases page depth and AEO surface area.

---

### 7. FAQ section

Add 3–5 Q&A pairs targeting secondary questions. Use H3 for each question. Each answer should be 2–4 sentences — specific enough to be used as a direct AI answer.

#### Q: [Secondary question 1]
[Answer]

#### Q: [Secondary question 2]
[Answer]

#### Q: [Secondary question 3]
[Answer]

---

### 8. Related resources

- Link to related service page
- Link to free resource or checklist download
- Link to related hub or cluster page
- Link to "Submit a Radiation Safety Question" if applicable

---

### 9. Call to action

Use a validation-safe CTA. Do not promise response times or guaranteed availability.

**Approved CTA options:**
- "Have a question about [topic]? Submit it using the form below — we review questions and provide general guidance where we can."
- "If you're working on [topic], contact us to discuss your situation."
- "Download our free [checklist name] to get started."

**Do not use:**
- "We will respond within X hours/days"
- "Guaranteed compliance support"
- "Available for immediate consultation"

## SEO/AEO quality checklist

Before publishing any page using this template, verify:

- [ ] Primary question answered in first 2–3 sentences
- [ ] H1 contains primary keyword
- [ ] Meta title under 60 chars with primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description 140–155 chars, answers the question
- [ ] At least one specific CFR citation
- [ ] Jurisdiction noted (NRC vs. Agreement State)
- [ ] FAQ section with 3+ Q&A pairs
- [ ] At least one internal link to a service page
- [ ] At least one internal link to a free resource or checklist
- [ ] CTA is validation-safe (no promises)
- [ ] Page fits one content cluster
- [ ] Slug matches primary keyword phrase
