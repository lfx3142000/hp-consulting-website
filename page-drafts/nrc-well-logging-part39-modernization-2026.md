# NRC Proposes Updates to 10 CFR Part 39 Well Logging Regulations

The NRC has initiated rulemaking to modernize 10 CFR Part 39, which governs the use of radioactive sources in well logging, tracer studies, and borehole applications in the oil and gas and geothermal industries.

## Background

Well logging is a specialized radiation safety field involving the use of sealed radioactive sources and radiation-generating equipment in boreholes to evaluate geological formations. Workers in this industry face unique radiation exposure scenarios — including remote locations, high-pressure environments, and frequent source transport. Part 39 has not been comprehensively updated in decades, and the NRC is now addressing accumulated modernization needs.

## Key Proposed Changes

**Updated source security requirements** — Proposed changes would align Part 39 source security provisions with current NRC physical security standards, including requirements for source accountability during field operations.

**Revised training and qualification standards** — The proposed rule includes updated minimum training requirements for well logging supervisors and assistants, reflecting modern radiation protection practices.

**Emergency response provisions** — New requirements would address lost or stuck source scenarios with clearer notification, search, and recovery obligations.

**Agreement State compatibility** — Updated compatibility designations would clarify which Part 39 requirements Agreement States must adopt versus which are discretionary.

**Electronic recordkeeping** — Modernization of recordkeeping requirements to explicitly permit electronic records and digital log systems.

## Who Is Affected?

- Oil and gas operators using radioactive sources or radiation-generating devices in exploration and production
- Well logging service companies and their supervisors and assistants
- Geothermal well operators
- Agreement State radiation control programs
- Companies providing source transport and logistics support for field operations

## Radiation Safety Considerations

Well logging presents distinct radiation safety challenges:

- Sources are transported frequently across state lines, requiring NRC or Agreement State license coordination
- Operations occur in remote settings with limited access to radiation safety support
- Source loss or stuck-source events require immediate notification and retrieval protocols
- Workers may accumulate dose from both sealed sources and x-ray generating equipment used in combination

## Timeline

The NRC issued a proposed rule for Part 39 modernization and accepted public comment. Final rulemaking is expected within the next 1–3 years, with Agreement State compatibility timelines following thereafter.
