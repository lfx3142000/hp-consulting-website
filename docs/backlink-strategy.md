## Tier 2 — Content-Based Backlinks (Earn via Publishing)

Create content that authoritative sites will naturally cite or link to.

### 2a. Data / Statistics Pages
- **"NRC Radiation Safety Inspection Deficiency Statistics [Year]"** — compile publicly available NRC inspection data into a summary page. News outlets and universities will cite it.
- **"RSO Salary and Job Market Report [Year]"** — aggregate BLS + job board data. HPS members and university programs link to these.
- **"State-by-State Radiation License Requirements"** — practical reference that regulators, universities, and employers link to frequently.

### 2b. Free Tools / Calculators
- Radiation dose calculator (mrem/mSv converter with ALARA context)
- RSO training checklist (downloadable PDF or interactive)
- NRC 10 CFR 19/20 quick-reference cheat sheet

### 2c. Guest Posts / Contributed Articles
Target publications that accept expert contributions:
- **Health Physics Society Newsletter / Journal** (hps.org) — submit a technical commentary
- **CRCPD (Conference of Radiation Control Program Directors)** — contribute to member resources
- **RadiologyInfo.org / RSNA** — submit radiation safety explainer articles
- **LinkedIn Articles** — publish thought leadership pieces linking back to site pages
- **Medium / Substack** — radiation safety explainers for general audience with site links
